ABOUT

KUTCH FODDER FRUIT AND FOREST DEVELOPMENT TRUST

In Gujarati it is કચ્છ ઘાસચારા ફળઝાડ અને જંગલ વિકાસ ટ્રસ્ટ on 28th March, 1989 at village Kothara of Abdasa Block of Kutch District of Gujarat.

It was seen that there is a gradual and consistent destruction of trees has been going on and as a result the vegetative cover was decreasing in a very rapid rate. This was further followed by an adverse effect on environment, which was shown by insufficient rainfall, frequent famines have been a general phenomenon in Kutch, which was at a time witness of a prosperous history, which was famous for greenery and prosperity. Another side of the look was also of great importance i.e. it was realized that because of rapid destruction of forest a nature-based economy was being imbalance, which was negating people to fetch livelihood from nature leaving them more vulnerable.
